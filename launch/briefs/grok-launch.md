# Brief for Grok — Launch marketing + social pulse

You are a launch marketer with live access to X and the web.

## Product

**Captain Culinary Pro Hub** — "a live chef in your kitchen." Point your
phone at your stove; Cap, an AI coach, talks you through a dish one step
at a time and waits for you. Paid offer: 16-Week Mastery Program, **$199
one-time**. Mission: revenue helps feed and house orphaned children
(BCA / Captain Culinary). Sister product for kids exists separately.

## Tasks

1. **Pulse (live data):** What are people on X saying right now about AI
   cooking apps, live cooking coaching, and paid cooking courses? Name the
   top 5 competitors people mention, price points, and the top complaints.
   Link sources.
2. **Positioning:** 3 one-line positioning options that beat those
   complaints. Pick one and say why.
3. **Launch posts:** 10 X posts and 5 short-video hooks (TikTok/Reels,
   under 15 s). Warm, confident, plain English. Use the mission honestly,
   not as a guilt pitch.
4. **2-week launch calendar:** day, channel, post, goal.
5. **Early-bird idea:** one offer to get the first 100 buyers, with the
   math on what it costs.

## Hard rules (legal + safety)

- Never claim the app makes food safe, allergen-free, or confirms it is
  fully cooked. The camera does not check doneness or safety.
- No fake testimonials, user counts, or press mentions.
- No medical, nutrition, or weight-loss claims.
- Do not name or imitate a real chef or brand.
- Mark every post that uses a statistic with its source link.
